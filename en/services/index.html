Station & airport transfers

Arrive relaxed, leave without waiting.

Your driver knows your actual arrival time before you do. He waits for you at the exit, with a sign bearing your name.

  • Flight or train tracking

    Give your flight or train number when you book: the pick-up time adjusts automatically if you are early or late.

  • Name-board greeting · €10

    On the platform or in the arrivals hall, your name clearly displayed. Ideal for guests or clients.

  • Help with luggage

    Suitcases carried to the V-Class’s boot: up to 6 large suitcases plus cabin bags.

  • 15 minutes’ free waiting

    Counted from your actual arrival time, then €0.50 per minute.

In one go, door to door.
