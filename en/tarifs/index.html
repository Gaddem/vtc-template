How your fare is calculated

A transparent calculation, in three steps

  • 1

    The zone sets the minimum

    €25 within Montpellier, €40 to the surrounding towns, up to 25 km.

  • 2

    Distance is added beyond that

    Each kilometre costs €2.20 as soon as that amount exceeds the zone minimum. For example, 22 km comes to €48.

  • 3

    Over 150 km: on quotation

    A firm, itemised price sent by email, with no obligation.
