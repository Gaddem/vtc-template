FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Booking, cancellation, waiting time, luggage, payment: everything you need to know before you get in.

Booking

How do I book a journey?

Online from the home page, in a minute: pick-up, drop-off, date and time, and the fixed fare appears straight away. You can also book by phone or WhatsApp. A confirmation is sent to you by text message and email.

How far in advance should I book?

Ideally the day before, and at least 2 hours before departure for an online booking. For a sooner departure, call directly: Antoine will tell you right away whether he is available.

Can I change my booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before pick-up, by phone, WhatsApp or email. If the journey changes, the fare is recalculated from the rates and confirmed to you before the ride.

How many passengers can you take?

Up to 7 passengers and 6 large suitcases, in a black Mercedes V-Class: ideal for a family or a small group. For a larger group, call us and we will find a suitable solution.

Cancellation

Can I cancel free of charge?

Yes, up to 24 hours before the pick-up time. Between 24 and 2 hours before: 50% of the fare. Less than 2 hours before, or if you do not show up: 100%. Read the terms of sale.

What if my flight or train is cancelled?

Cancellation is then free of charge, whatever the notice, on presentation of the carrier’s proof.

Waiting time

How long will the driver wait?

15 minutes are free after the scheduled time. Beyond that, waiting costs €0.50 per minute and you are notified by text message.

My flight or train is late: will I pay for waiting?

No. Thanks to the number given when you book, your arrival is tracked in real time: the 15 free minutes start from your actual arrival.

Luggage and pets

How much luggage can I bring?

Up to 6 large suitcases plus cabin bags: the Mercedes V-Class is a long-wheelbase van. For more, let us know when you book and we will suggest a suitable solution.

What counts as oversized luggage?

A bike, golf bag, skis, double buggy, musical instrument… It costs €5 per item and must be mentioned when you book.

Can I travel with my pet?

Yes, for €5, in a bag or travel crate. Guide and assistance dogs travel free of charge.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card or cash on board, or online payment through a secure link sent by text message. Businesses can also pay by bank transfer on receipt of invoice.

Can businesses pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Tick “Booking for a company” when you book, then choose “Bank transfer on receipt of invoice”. With a business account, the month’s journeys are grouped on a single invoice, paid by transfer within 30 days. Request a business account.

Can the fare change after I book?

No. The quoted fare is the fare you pay, with no surge pricing. Only the extras you asked for and waiting beyond the 15 free minutes are added. See the rates.

Invoices

Will I receive an invoice?

Yes, automatically by email after each journey, with the VAT itemised.

Can I get an invoice in my company’s name?

Yes: tick “Booking for a company” and enter the company name. With a business account, you receive a monthly summary invoice.

Promo codes

Do you offer promo codes?

Our fares are already set as fairly as possible and the same at any hour. Occasional codes may be sent to regular clients or offered at events.

How do I use a promo code?

Enter it at the last step of your booking: the discount appears in the summary before you confirm. One code per journey, not combinable.

Child seats

Do you provide a child seat?

Yes, on request and free of charge: baby seat, child seat or booster. Give the child’s age and weight when you book.

Up to what age is a suitable seat required?

In France, children under 10 must travel in a seat or booster suited to their height and weight. The equipment is provided: just ask.
